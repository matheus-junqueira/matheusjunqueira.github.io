I am a Predoctoral Fellow at the Global Research Training Initiative (GRTI-UPPER) at Johns Hopkins SAIS, working with Professors Filipe Campante and Shoumitro Chatterjee on projects in political economy, international trade, and development.

Before joining GRTI, I earned an M.A. in Business Economics from FGV-EESP and a B.A. in Economics from FEA-USP. My research uses microdata and quasi-experimental methods to study how political institutions shape economic outcomes.
